# Support registry: open findings

Defects and open questions found while converting the support system to derive
from `src/supports/supportTypeRegistry.ts`. **Only live items are here.**

Resolved findings are archived at `lysdiag/plan-history/findings-resolved.md`
(outside the repo). A handful of fixed ones stay below, in *Lessons*, because
they changed how work here is verified.

**Status values.** `open` is a live defect or an undecided question.
`recheck` tests clean but changes visible behaviour, so it wants a second pair
of eyes. `partly fixed` means the seam landed and call sites remain.

**Record who decided, and on what evidence.** An entry with neither is not a
decision -- mark it open. A previous revision closed a finding on
"confirmed by the owner" with no such confirmation on record, and every later
pass then skipped it deliberately.
